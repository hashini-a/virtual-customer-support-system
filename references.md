# References

The following resources can be used for understanding virtual customer support systems, CRM, databases, and IT service management.

## 1. CRM Concepts

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) concepts were used to understand customer interaction, support management, and feedback handling.

## 2. IT Service Management

IT service management concepts were used to understand ticket management, incident handling, service requests, and support workflows.

## 3. Database Management

Database management concepts were used to design the proposed Student, Ticket, Support Staff, Knowledge Base, and Feedback entities.

## 4. Virtual Customer Support

General virtual customer support concepts were used to understand online support portals, knowledge bases, ticket tracking, and user communication.

## 5. User Experience

Basic user-interface and user-experience principles were considered to make the proposed system simple and easy to use for students.

## 6. System Design

Basic software system architecture and workflow concepts were used to design the overall structure and interaction between the system modules.

## Note

The proposed Virtual Customer Support System is a conceptual academic design. The architecture, workflows, database structure, and implementation plan are intended as a practical roadmap for a future implementation.
